Common questions

The things most owners ask first

What if it doesn't know the answer to something?

It's built to say a team member will follow up rather than guess or make something up — the same rule that applies to your real script. A true emergency can also be routed straight to whoever you're already on-call to for it.

What happens if we go over our plan's minutes?

Nothing changes for the customer on the phone — calls and chats are never cut off or slowed down. Extra minutes beyond your plan are simply billed automatically at £0.30–£0.35/minute and added to your next invoice.

Will it sound robotic, or will customers know it's AI?

It always discloses that it's an AI assistant right in the greeting — that's a compliance requirement, not optional. Beyond that, you write and approve every word it says before it ever talks to a real customer, so the tone is yours, not a generic script.

What happens to our current phone number?

Your AI agent gets its own live number once you're ready for phone calls, and you decide how it fits your existing line — most businesses just forward their current number to it, so nothing about the number your customers already call has to change.

Can we cancel or switch back easily?

Yes — cancel any time from your own self-service account, no phone call or long-term contract required. Cancel during your 14-day free trial and you're never charged at all; cancel after that and billing stops immediately, with access until the end of what you already paid for.

What if I'm not happy after my trial?

You're covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee: if it's not working out within 30 days of your first charge, tell us and we'll refund that payment in full, no questions asked. After that, cancelling any time simply stops future billing.

Can we review what it will say before it goes live?

Always. Every script is drafted from your own answers, and you read, edit, and approve it before it's ever used on a real call or chat — the same review applies any time you edit it yourself later.

What actually happens when the AI spots something urgent?

The moment it recognizes a true emergency — based on your own definition from your intake form — it alerts you immediately: a text to the on-call number you provide, or an email to your account address if you haven't set one up. It's never just logged for you to find later, and the caller is told it's been flagged as urgent.

What if my business is seasonal?

There's no penalty for pausing. Cancel any time, with no contract and no setup fee to redo, and come back whenever your season picks back up — most seasonal businesses simply resubscribe each year rather than paying through a quiet season.
