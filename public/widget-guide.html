Add your chat widget to your website

You already have your widget code — you copied it from the "Website chat widget code" box on your dashboard. It's one line and looks something like <script src="..." data-client-id="..."></script>. Pick whichever platform your website is built on below, and follow the steps. If you're not sure what your website is built on, that's okay — whoever set it up for you will know, or you can send them the "Other / I'm not sure" instructions and they'll take it from there.

Don't have your code yet? Go to your dashboard, find your business's card, and look for the "Website chat widget code" box — it only appears once you're on a paid plan. Copy the whole line, then come back here.

WordPress

1

Log into your WordPress admin area — usually yourwebsite.com/wp-admin.

2

On the left menu, go to Plugins → Add New. Search for a free plugin called "WPCode" (or "Insert Headers and Footers" if WPCode isn't available). Click Install Now, then Activate.

3

A new menu item will appear — look for Code Snippets → Header & Footer (naming varies slightly by plugin, but it will mention "header" and "footer").

4

Paste your widget code into the "Footer" box (not the header box).

5

Click Save or Update.

6

Open your live website in a new tab. You should see a "Chat with us" bubble in the bottom corner. Done!

Wix

1

Log into Wix and open the Editor for your site.

2

Go to Settings, then find "Custom Code" (you can also type "custom code" into the settings search box).

3

Click "+ Add Custom Code" and paste your widget code into the box.

4

Set it to load on "All Pages", and set the placement to "Body - end".

5

Click Apply, then click Publish in the top right so the change goes live.

6

Open your live website. You should see a "Chat with us" bubble in the bottom corner. Done!

Squarespace

1

Log into Squarespace and open your site.

2

Go to Settings → Advanced → Code Injection.

3

Paste your widget code into the "Footer" box.

4

Click Save.

5

Open your live website. You should see a "Chat with us" bubble in the bottom corner. Done!

Code Injection is only available on Squarespace's Business plan or higher. If you don't see that option, your current plan may not support it — Squarespace support can confirm.

Other website, or not sure what it's built on

1

Copy your widget code from your dashboard.

2

Send it to whoever built or manages your website, along with this instruction: "Please paste this code just before the closing </body> tag, on every page of the site."

3

Most website builders also have a section for this that a developer will recognize immediately — often called "Custom code," "Tracking code," "Footer scripts," or similar. For example, Shopify calls it "Edit code," and Webflow calls it "Custom Code" under Project Settings.

4

Once it's added, a "Chat with us" bubble should appear in the bottom corner of the live site. No other setup is needed on our end.

Not seeing the chat bubble? Try a hard refresh of the page (hold Shift and click reload, or clear your browser cache). Make sure you copied the entire code snippet, starting with <script and ending with </script>. The widget also only works while your subscription is active on a paid plan — if you recently cancelled, that's expected. Still stuck? Reply to any email from us and we'll help directly.
