DispatchAI ← Back to home Privacy Policy Last updated: 27 October 2026 This policy explains what personal data Dispatch AI collects, why, and what we do with it. "Dispatch AI", "we" and "us" means Christopher Holt, trading as Dispatch AI. If you're a business that subscribes to Dispatch AI, we refer to you as "you" or "the client" below; if you're one of your customers whose call or message was handled by a client's AI agent, we refer to you as "your customer" or "the end customer". In plain English: we collect the information needed to run your AI phone and chat agent — your business details, and the calls, chats and bookings your agent handles for your customers — and we only share it with the specific other companies (like our AI, phone and payment providers) that make the service work. We don't sell personal data, ever. 1. Two roles we play with data Dispatch AI handles two different kinds of personal data, and UK GDPR treats them differently: • Your business's own data (your name, company details, contact email, billing information) — here, we're the data controller: we decide why and how it's used, as described in this policy. • Your customers' data, collected while your AI agent answers their calls or chats and books their appointments — here, we're a data processor acting only on your instructions, and you (the client business) are the controller. You're responsible for having a lawful basis to let us process your customers' data on your behalf; we only use it to provide the service to you. 2. What we collect From you, as our client • Your name, company name, email address and phone number. • Account and billing details, including subscription status and payment history (card details themselves are handled directly by Stripe — we never see or store your full card number). • The script, pricing and service details you give us to build your AI agent. • Any third-party accounts you choose to connect, such as Google Calendar or ServiceM8. On your behalf, about your customers • Names, phone numbers and email addresses of customers who call or message your AI agent. • Call recordings and transcripts, and website chat transcripts. • Appointment and job details (what was booked, when, and any notes taken). 3. Why we use it • To operate the service: answering calls and chats, booking appointments, sending missed-call text-backs, and showing you a record of it all in your client portal. • To bill you correctly, including tracking call-and-chat minutes against your plan. • To provide support, and to email you about your account, usage, or important changes to the service. • To improve the service — for example, understanding common questions your agent is asked, so we can make it better. Our legal basis for this is performing our contract with you, and our (and your) legitimate interests in running the service properly. We don't use your customers' data for our own marketing, and we don't sell personal data to anyone. 4. Who we share it with We share data with a small number of specialist providers, each only to the extent needed to provide their part of the service, and under their own data protection terms: Provider What they handle Anthropic (Claude) Generates your AI agent's chat responses and scripts. Retell AI Powers the voice side of your AI phone agent. Telnyx Your phone number, call routing and text messages. Stripe Payment processing and billing. Resend Sending transactional emails (notifications, receipts). Google Calendar Only if you connect it — syncing bookings to your calendar. ServiceM8 Only if you connect it — syncing jobs to your ServiceM8 account. Some of these providers are based outside the UK (for example, in the United States). Where that's the case, we rely on the safeguards they provide (such as standard contractual clauses) for the data to be transferred lawfully. 5. How long we keep it We keep your account data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you resubscribe or for legal/accounting reasons. Call recordings, transcripts and booking details are kept for as long as they're useful to you (visible in your portal) and are deleted or anonymised once they're no longer needed for the service or a legal requirement. 6. Cookies and tracking Our website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. We only use the minimum necessary to make the site work (for example, keeping you signed in to your client portal). 7. Security We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our systems. No system is completely risk-free, but we take security seriously and work with reputable providers who do the same. 8. Your rights Under UK GDPR, you (or, where we're a processor, your customer via you) have the right to ask for access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal data we hold, and to object to or restrict certain uses of it. To exercise these rights, get in touch through your client portal or the contact details on our website. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). 9. Children Dispatch AI is a business service and isn't directed at, or knowingly used by, children. 10. Changes to this policy We may update this policy as the service changes. If we make a material change, we'll let you know by email or through your client portal with reasonable notice. 11.
