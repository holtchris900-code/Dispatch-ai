Your past customers

Upload a list of your past customers and this page will watch for anyone who's due for a reminder about their next service -- and draft a short, friendly message for you to look over before it goes anywhere. Nothing is ever sent without you approving it first.

Add customers

Upload a CSV (a spreadsheet saved as "CSV" from Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers) with these columns: name, email, phone, serviceType, lastServiceDate, remindAfterMonths. Only name is required, but without an email we won't be able to send anything to that person. lastServiceDate should look like 2025-04-15. remindAfterMonths is optional -- how many months after their last service to check back in; if left blank we'll use 6 months.
